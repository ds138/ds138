### Daniel Schweizer

Data scientist (PhD) at [Fraunhofer EMI](https://www.emi.fraunhofer.de/) in Freiburg, Germany.
I work on the reliability of learning systems: how far can we trust a model's prediction,
and how do we quantify its uncertainty?

**What I have worked on**
- Uncertainty quantification for deep learning: ensembles, Monte Carlo dropout, conformal prediction
- Time series analysis and forecasting
- Explainable anomaly detection and semantic segmentation of satellite imagery
- Numerical simulation and signal processing, from my background in the geosciences
